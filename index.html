What we carry

The showroom is small but the range isn't. Most of what's on the boards can be in your driveway within the week; the rest we special order.

Porcelain & ceramic

Large format, wood look, classic subway. Floors and walls, in stock and special order.

Mosaics & backsplash

Glass, marble hex, zellige and hand-glazed pieces for backsplashes and shower floors.

Natural stone

Marble, quartzite and granite. We'll walk the racks with you and tag the slab you pick.

Countertops

Quartz and stone counters, templated and cut to your kitchen. Sinks and faucets too.

Cabinetry

Kitchen and vanity cabinets in standard and custom sizes, with drawings before you order.

Shower & bath

Vanities, shower doors, niches, trim pieces. The parts nobody thinks about until the plumber asks.

The store

Run by two people who will actually sit with you

Tiles Up is owned by Rasha and Kamal, a husband and wife team. It's the kind of store where the person helping you pick a backsplash is the same person who prices it and answers the phone when your contractor has a question.

Rasha handles most of the design work. She'll pull boards, sketch the layout, and send you home with sample boards to hold against your counters in your own light. Kamal keeps the orders and deliveries honest. When a contractor miscounted mid-job, they've been known to drive the extra box over themselves.

Interior designers on Long Island send their clients here. So do neighbors who wandered in off Route 110. Both get the same treatment.

How a project usually goes

No appointment needed, though calling ahead never hurts.

  1. 1

    Bring what you have

    Photos on your phone, rough measurements, a countertop chip, a Pinterest board. Any of it helps.

  2. 2

    Sit down and pull samples

    Rasha will lay options on the desk and talk through layout. Take sample boards home to check them in your light.

  3. 3

    Get a straight price

    Have a quote from another store? Bring it. Matching or beating it is a point of pride here.

  4. 4

    Delivered to your door

    Orders arrive wrapped and counted. If your installer runs short mid-job, call us and we'll sort it out.
